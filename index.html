A Second Life for Your Clothes

At Rethread, we believe that every garment has value — not just in how it looks, but in the memories it holds and the resources it represents.

Our journey began with a simple idea: to reduce textile waste and give clothes a longer, more meaningful life through repair, restyle and reuse.
